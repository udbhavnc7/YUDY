# YUDY — One room

## 1. Concept

One photorealistic room is Udbhav NC’s portfolio. The furniture carries the content. The camera eventually moves through authored views of the room as the visitor scrolls.

## 2. Why the lamp comes first

Adding furniture magnifies weak silhouettes, flat materials and poor lighting. An isolated object exposes those problems while each cause can still be tested. Calibrate the renderer with a grey box before judging the lamp.

## 3. Materials

A turned wooden body, a pleated shade and small fittings can be built from geometry. Use real surface textures and an environment with reflected light. Future upholstery, plants and close-up books need sourced or generated meshes.

## 4. Interaction

The lamp turns on and reveals the name in its light. Visitors can switch it with the object or a keyboard-accessible control. A narrow viewport keeps the complete object in frame.

## 5. Scope

Phase 0 contains this lamp and the surfaces that catch its light. Later furniture, portfolio content, scroll travel and the accessible work route follow the roadmap.

## 6. Visual review

Inspect the running scene with the lamp on and off. Look at the weave, shade edges, brass reflections, wood grain, contact shadow and falloff on the surrounding surfaces. Compare the scene with a photograph of a lamp in a dark room. Continue refining the asset, material or lighting until the object passes that test before starting the room.
