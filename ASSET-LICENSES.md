## Rules

- No film stills, clips, lyrics, uploaded copyrighted audio, or scraped photos.
- Fonts self-hosted and subset; record licence and version. Two handwriting
  faces are needed: one for your annotations, one for hers.
- AI-generated meshes logged with the tool and prompt used.
- Named songs appear only as official Spotify or YouTube embeds.
- No library that phones home. Law 6 applies to dependencies too.
